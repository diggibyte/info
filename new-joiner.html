Onboarding Steps

Step 1
Register on the Databricks Partner Academy
  1. Go to the Databricks Partner Academy.
  2. Sign up with your Diggibyte corporate email ID only. Personal email IDs are not linked to our partner account, and your progress will not count toward Diggibyte's partner credentials.
  3. Select "Diggibyte" as your partner organisation when prompted.
  4. Confirm your registration to the L&D team, and report any access issues to Hari Vignesh.
Step 2
Core Data Engineering

Courses:

  1. Data Engineer Learning Plan | Duration: 2 hours
  2. Data Ingestion with Lakeflow Connect | Duration: Self-paced
  3. Advanced Techniques with Apache Spark Declarative Pipelines | Duration: 2 hours
Outcome: You will understand the Databricks Lakehouse, how to ingest data from files, databases and SaaS sources using Lakeflow Connect, and how to build production-grade declarative pipelines.
Step 3
DevOps & Automated Deployment

Courses:

  1. DevOps Essentials for Data Engineering | Duration: Self-paced
  2. Automated Deployment with Declarative Automation Bundles | Duration: 2 hours
Outcome: You will be able to apply CI/CD, version control and bundle-based deployment practices, which we use as standard on customer projects.
Step 4
Generative AI with Genie

Courses:

  1. Building Reliable Conversational Agents with Genie | Duration: Self-paced
  2. Genie One – Genie Agents Foundations | Duration: Self-paced
Outcome: You will be able to design and build conversational analytics experiences on Databricks, a growing area in our customer engagements.
Step 5
Partner Instructor-Led Training

Databricks Partner Academy offers a hybrid learning approach. Alongside on-demand training, you can attend workshops led by Databricks expert trainers. These prepare you to earn your GTM/Sales and Delivery accreditations and certifications.

  1. Review the Partner Training Calendar.
  2. Enrol in at least one relevant ILT session (Data Engineering or Generative AI recommended).
  3. Share the session name and date with hari.vignesh@diggibyte.com and your reporting manager before you enrol, so that project schedules can be aligned.

Note: If no suitable session falls within your first week, enrol in the earliest available one and inform L&D.

Step 6
Track & Confirm CompletionDay 10
  1. Update your course completion details in the CoE App.
  2. Inform your reporting manager of completion.
  3. Next step: L&D will help you schedule the Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate exam.
